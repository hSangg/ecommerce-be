Root cause: It's not my app failing to process requests, it's a TCP accept-queue overflow at startup burst,
not runtime performance issue.

server:
  tomcat:
    accept-count: 100

accept-count is the OS-level listen backlog. When 150 client sockets try to connect at the same time, the OS
will only hold 100 connections (Window TCP stack) and the rest will be dropped.

What we can do?
1. Increase the accept-count in configuration.

Why big platform can handle 1 million requests per second?
My case: 1 process, 1 listen socket, backlog = 100, 150 simultaneous.

Their case:
- They use load balancers to distribute the load across multiple processes and servers.
- OS-level tuning.
